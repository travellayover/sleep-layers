# SleepAlb false-lead hooks

This is the pattern that was meant.

The hook starts **wrong on purpose** — close to a scare, a rumor, a sentence that would get someone to stop:

- sleeping can put you in a coma

Then the **story unsells it**. Sleep does not do that. The info lives in the body, not in the first line.

That is the gaslight: three seconds of a false night. Then we take it back and tell the real one.

If the hook already says "not really" or "here's why that's a myth," there is nothing to watch.

---

## The shape

```
HOOK     almost-wrong. scare, rumor, dare, or mix-up.
         sounds like it might be true.
         does not wink.

STORY    1. unsell: that is not true / not the whole night
         2. the mix-up in one breath (what people confuse)
         3. one real mechanism you can stand behind
         4. one try-tonight from an existing guide
         5. what this cannot promise
```

Example, coma:

| Beat | Line |
|---|---|
| Hook | sleeping can put you in a coma |
| Unsell | it cannot. sleep is not a coma. |
| Mix-up | people use "out cold" and "dead asleep" like they are medical. they are not. |
| Real info | sleep is an active night. you still move through stages. you can be woken. a coma is a different state. |
| Tonight | one change that is actually about *this* night — light, hours, a diary — not a scare |
| Cannot promise | we are not diagnosing why someone sleeps a lot or cannot wake. that is a clinician. |

The viewer came for the coma. They stay for the distinction. They leave with one useful night move. If they leave still believing the hook, the post failed.

This is the Debunk carousel type already in the critic engine (9–12 slides). Same spine for a 15-second Reel.

## Why the hook has to start different

A true, polite opener teaches in the title and dies.

- Sleep is not the same as a coma  
- 5 differences between sleep and unconsciousness  
- Why 8 hours does not mean you are broken  

An almost-wrong opener **borrows the scare** and makes the story the only place the truth fits:

- sleeping can put you in a coma  
- 8 hours is a soft coma  
- my eyes are red because I did not come all the way back  
- why should I sleep if sleep is just leaving  

The first line is allowed to sound like a rumor the comments already believe. The body is not allowed to keep the rumor.

---

## How close to the lie

| Too soft | Close enough | Too far |
|---|---|---|
| Sleep and coma are different | sleeping can put you in a coma | sleep will put you in a coma and here is how |
| Trackers are imperfect | your watch thinks you were gone | your watch proves you have a disease |
| Morning face is common | my eyes are red because I did not wake up all the way | red eyes mean you are dying |
| Waking at 3 happens | 3:07 is the hour you almost do not come back | 3am means you have a disorder |

Close enough = a person could believe it for one scroll.  
Too far = we assert a harm, a diagnosis, a method, or a guarantee.

Hard-list words still fail the finished caption: cure, treat, heal, prevent, remedy, miracle, diagnose, guarantee, clinically proven. The hook can voice a **myth**. The story cannot turn the myth into advice.

We never leave "sleeping leads to comas" as the takeaway. We never tell someone who cannot wake to ignore care. If the real story is "this can be bigger than a bad night," land on `when-professional-support-is-appropriate` as a closer, not as a hook stinger.

---

## Hold the false lead (so they watch)

```
0:00  the almost-wrong line. no smile. no "myth:" label.
0:03  one more turn of the screw. (I slept 11 hours and I still felt gone.)
0:06  unsell. "that's not what this is."
0:09  the mix-up + one real fact.
0:12  one tonight move. what this cannot promise.
```

Kills the pattern:

- putting "not true" in the hook
- a smirk in the first frame
- teaching the distinction before they want it
- ending on the scare
- a new lie to replace the old one ("actually cortisol," "actually your liver")

The viewer should think: *wait, is that real?* then *oh. that's not what it is.* then *so what do I do tonight?*

## Two seeds, same spine

**Scare seed — coma**

- sleeping can put you in a coma  
- sleep is just a coma we agreed to  
- if I sleep too long I will not wake up  
- the difference between sleep and a coma is marketing  

Story unsells: not a coma. sleep is reversible, staged, wakeable. "Out cold" is language, not a diagnosis. If someone cannot be woken, that is not a sleep tip.

**Private seed — eyes / why sleep**

- my eyes are red after sleep  
- my eyes are red because I did not come all the way back  
- why should I sleep if I just go away  

Story unsells: red eyes are not leftover unconsciousness. Morning face has boring causes (dry air, salt, heat, position, the hours you actually got). "Why should I sleep" is a real mood, not proof that sleep is optional. Then one guide move.

Same machine. Different costume.

## Breeding more false leads

Take a true, boring fact. Write the **rumor a tired person would make from it.** That rumor is the hook. The fact is the story.

| Boring true | False lead (hook) | Story must say |
|---|---|---|
| Sleep is not a coma | sleeping can put you in a coma | it cannot; here is the mix-up |
| You can be woken from sleep | if I go too deep I will not come back | deep is not unwakeable; here is what "heavy" is |
| Trackers guess motion | my watch says I was gone | the watch cannot see a coma or a soul; it sees movement |
| Morning eyelids can look worse | sleep ruined my eyes | sleep did not bill your eyes; here's a tonight variable |
| 8 hours is a population average | 8 hours will put you under | the number is not a spell |
| A 3am wake is common | 3:07 is the death of the night | it is a clock, not a verdict |
| Long time in bed is not the same as sleep | I disappeared for 10 hours | hours are an alibi; a diary is ruder and more useful |
| Naps can be short | a nap is a test coma | a nap is a short sleep; it is not a medical event |

If you cannot write the unsell in one sentence, the lead is too vague or too dangerous. Drop it.

## Operator rule

A false-lead hook is unfinished content. Do not post the line alone. Do not put it on a Pin without the unsell in the description. Do not let a first frame travel as a screenshot.

The bank in `1000-hooks.md` is **leads only**. Each family lists the unsell the story owes.

Finished assets still pass L0. Educational. Not medical advice. We are the people who take the scare back.
