# SleepAlb — 1,000 false-lead hooks

The hook starts **almost wrong**. The story unsells it. The info lives in the body.

Example: `sleeping can put you in a coma` → it cannot. Sleep is not a coma. Then the mix-up. Then one tonight move.

These lines are **leads only**. Do not post a screenshot of the first frame as a fact. Each family names the unsell the story owes.

Craft: [`02-gaslight-texture.md`](./02-gaslight-texture.md). How a post ships: [`00-sleepalb-direction-strategy.md`](./00-sleepalb-direction-strategy.md).

Do not run L0 critic on this file. Hooks are not captions.

**Count:** 1000 unique lines. 25 families × 40.
