# SleepAlb curiosity craft

The last doc said: sound like the comment, not the course.

This one is the **skill** under that. How to invent more patterns, and how to make a line hard enough that someone watches instead of nodding and scrolling.

A nod is not a watch. Relatable is cheap. Curiosity is a debt.

---

## What makes a person watch

They watch when the first line opens a **gap** their own night cannot close.

```
watch heat  =  (information gap  ×  contradiction  ×  "that's my night")
               ÷  how fast the line already explains itself
```

| If the viewer… | They do this | Heat |
|---|---|---|
| Feels seen, already knows the ending | Like, skip | Low |
| Feels lectured | Skip | Dead |
| Feels a private fact that should not be true | Stay | High |
| Can finish your sentence before you do | Skip | Dead |

`why should I sleep` works because it is a question you are not supposed to ask.
`my eyes are red after sleep` works because sleep was supposed to fix the face, and the face got worse.

Both leave a stone in the shoe. The video is the only place the stone comes out.

If you explain the red eyes in the same sentence as the hook, you spent the curiosity in the title. There is nothing left to watch.

## The 12 mother patterns

Every SleepAlb opener is a child of one of these. Learn the mothers. Breed from them. Do not memorize 1,000 lines as the skill.

### 1. Information gap

Show a result. Hide the cause.

- my eyes are red after sleep
- I woke up thirstier than I went to bed

The brain has to know why. Do not say why yet.

### 2. Broken deal

They paid. They did not receive.

- I slept 8 hours and I look worse
- I did the whole routine and my brain still clocked in

The deal (hours, routine, darkness, a tracker score) is the setup. The unpaid morning is the hook.

### 3. Forbidden question

Ask the thing a good adult is not allowed to ask.

- why should I sleep
- do I even need tonight

Curiosity here is social: can someone say this out loud.

### 4. False finish

They thought the night was over. It was not.

- I finally slept and my face is angry
- I was out and then I was not

The word *finally* or *almost* is a tripwire. Keep it.

### 5. Clock ghost

A specific time feels like evidence, not a tip.

- why is it always 3:07
- I do not remember deciding to be awake at 5:47

Vague "middle of the night" is a lecture. 3:07 is a memory.

### 6. Almost

An action that did not complete.

- I was almost gone and then my jaw clenched
- I hit sleepy on the couch and woke up in the hallway of my own bed

Open loops are older than short-form. Do not close them in the first frame.

### 7. Object leftover

One thing from the day is still in the night.

- the 2pm coffee is still in my eyes
- the laptop is closed and the meeting is not

An object is a villain you can film. "Stress" is not.

### 8. Someone else can

Two bodies, one night, unfair.

- they are gone in 40 seconds
- my kid sleeps in the car and I cannot sleep in a bed

Social comparison is curiosity with a bruise. Stay observational. No mockery of the other person.

### 9. Score liar

A number congratulated them. The body did not.

- my watch said great
- 92 and I still cannot see my own face

The gap is between dashboard and bathroom mirror.

### 10. Identity crack

The person they think they are does not match the night.

- I used to fall asleep in cars
- I am not a night person and I am still awake

Identity is stickier than a tip. Do not turn it into a brand ("girlies who can't sleep"). Keep it first person.

### 11. Unsaid want

The real desire is not sleep.

- I do not want to sleep I want the house to stay mine
- I am protecting the only quiet I get

This is the deepest SleepAlb line. It is why "why should I sleep" hits. The night is the last private room.

### 12. Proof before name

Show the evidence. Do not title it a sleep tip.

- [first frame: red eyes, no caption that says "tired"]
- I look like I fought my pillow

If the on-screen text could be a Pinterest category, you named it too early.

## The breeding skill (how you get more patterns)

Take any seed that already has heat. Run it through **eight rotations**. Each child must still leave a gap.

Seed: `my eyes are red after sleep`

| Rotation | Move | Child |
|---|---|---|
| Body | Swap the evidence | my jaw is sore and I was "asleep" |
| Clock | Pin a time | I woke at 6 looking like 3 |
| Broken deal | Name what they paid | I paid 8 hours and my eyes still look open |
| Almost / false finish | The night almost worked | I finally slept and my eyes got worse |
| Other person | Unfair pair | they look rested and I look like the night happened to me |
| Object | Leave a culprit | the salt, the heat, the wine, something stayed in my face |
| Score | Dashboard vs mirror | my watch is proud of a face I do not recognize |
| Reverse the want | They wanted the opposite | I wanted sleep to fix my face and it billed me instead |

Then raise heat. Ask only these four checks:

1. **Can they finish the sentence?** If yes, cut a word or add a contradiction.
2. **Did you explain it?** If the cause is in the hook, move the cause to second 8.
3. **Is it a night someone had?** If it is generic tired, add a body, a clock, or an object.
4. **Would they search this or frame this?** Search = why / my / after. Frame = bathroom evidence.

If a rotation loses the gap, throw it away. Volume is not the skill. Breeding until the gap hurts is the skill.

## Hold the gap (so they actually watch)

The hook is only the debt. The first 12 seconds have to refuse to pay it too fast.

```
0:00  muttered line on screen. nothing else.
0:02  sharpen the contradiction. do not teach.
0:06  one mechanism or reframe. still no list.
0:10  one try-tonight.
end   what this cannot promise + soft next step
```

Kills curiosity on contact:

- "here's why" in the first line
- a number of tips in the hook
- naming the lesson (`sleep hygiene`, `circadian`, `cortisol hack`)
- answering the red eyes in the same breath
- a mattress-ad voice
- POV templates that already tell the joke

The viewer should think: *wait, that happens to me, and I do not know why either.*

## Worked breeding (watch-hard)

**Seed A — forbidden question**

- why should I sleep
- why should I sleep if I just lie there
- why should I sleep when the house is finally mine
- why should I close a day I did not get to live
- I am not tired of being awake I am tired of the performance of going to bed

Last line is hottest: it names the real want and still does not explain what to do.

**Seed B — aftermath face**

- my eyes are red after sleep
- I slept and my eyes look more awake than I am
- sleep did something to my eyes and it was not rest
- I went to bed with a face and woke up with last night on it
- why do my eyes look like they worked the night shift

Last line is hottest: it assigns a job to the eyes without diagnosing anything.

**Seed C — score liar**

- my watch said I slept great
- 8 hours, 92, and a face that looks unconvinced
- my ring is in love with a night I do not remember
- the app celebrated and my eyes did not get the memo

Do not add "because REM" in the hook. That is a payment.

## Operator drill (10 minutes)

1. Pick one mother.
2. Write 8 rotations from one seed.
3. Kill any line the viewer can finish.
4. Kill any line that contains the lesson.
5. Keep the two that would make *you* tap if they were not yours.
6. Only then look at `1000-hooks.md` to steal a cousin, not a copy.

The bank is a greenhouse. The skill is the breeding.
